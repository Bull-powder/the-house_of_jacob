House of Jacob Community Rules

We want this to be a welcoming place to connect, share, and grow. By using the community, agree to follow these rules:

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate, or targeted abuse.
  2. Protect privacy. Do not publish another person's private details, messages, or images without permission.
  3. Share responsibly. Do not post illegal content, scams, impersonation, or deliberately misleading material.
  4. Respect creative work. Only upload media you own or have permission to use. Online music previews may be limited by the provider.
  5. Keep content appropriate. Do not share exploitative, sexually abusive, or graphic violent content.
  6. No spam or manipulation. Avoid repeated unsolicited promotions, fake engagement, and deceptive follower schemes.
  7. Use reporting and verification honestly. Verification is reviewed; follower count alone does not grant a badge.
  8. Respect live-stream safety. Do not expose private information, threaten people, or stream content that violates these rules.

Content may be removed and accounts may be restricted for serious or repeated violations. If something feels unsafe, report it to the community moderators.
